Regressor effectiveness and comparison with P1

The development-selected specification is etf only (1 raw input(s), versus 29 in the full regression). On 335,775 July–September targets under the 15-minute reporting assumption, its MAE is 19.848 bp, versus 19.944 for the full regression and 19.907 for the earlier P1 formula applied to the same pipeline's time-only anchor.

On 131,752 identical targets shared with the saved earlier P1, using zero delay, selected-model MAE is 19.073 bp versus 17.990 bp for earlier P1. This matched-target comparison still retains differences in filtering history and calibration.

Evaluation design

January–February initialize training. All candidate specifications use expanding monthly fits and identical March–June development observations. Selection minimizes development MAE, then chooses the fewest raw inputs within a fixed 0.10 bp tolerance of that minimum. Interaction-category count and MAE break ties. The choice is written to selection.json before any July–September candidate assessment. The same choice is used under both reporting-delay assumptions. July–September coefficients are refitted each month using available earlier labels. These dates were inspected in previous research, so this is a chronological retrospective assessment, not a fresh untouched holdout.

The fixed penalty remains alpha=100; no penalty, half-life, time window, or subgroup threshold is tuned in this study. Both states and targets retain 10:00 ≤ time < 15:30 Eastern. Sector and rating snapshots remain retrospective. Errors are 10,000 × (predicted/observed transaction price − 1), averaged across targets; they are not strategy returns.

Interpretation and next steps

Keep the earlier P1 as a benchmark unless the later matched comparisons support replacement. Retain a regressor group for its incremental later predictive benefit, not a large fitted coefficient. If a simpler specification improves MAE but worsens RMSE, it trades typical error against sensitivity to large misses. A predeclared subgroup result can motivate a future conditional model, but a routing rule designed after these results needs a new period for validation. Useful next inputs remain verified historical sector/rating labels, actual TRACE dissemination times and intraday rate observations with verified timestamps. Duration is a maturity cash-flow proxy, not effective duration for callable bonds. The target remains a noisy transaction price rather than directly observed fair value.

Reproduction and artifacts

Run python3 scripts/corporate_bonds/run_regressor_study.py. The source feature panels are immutable and hash-verified. specifications.json records every feature set; selection.json freezes the choice; development_predictions.parquet and assessment_predictions_*.parquet preserve forecasts; coefficients.csv and models/ preserve fitted models. Group tables, paired intervals, diagnostics, fold audits and manifest hashes accompany this report.
